# LULU — Global Investment Society | Investment Research Assignment

Deliverables for the GIS IR selection assignment on **lululemon athletica inc. (NASDAQ: LULU)**.
Everything here was **built from scratch** (no CFI or third-party templates): the Excel
models are generated by Python (`openpyxl`) and the pitch deck by Python (`python-pptx`).

## Recommendation

**OVERWEIGHT / LONG** — 12-month price target **$140** (~40% upside vs ~$100).

Thesis in one line: a net-cash, ~20%-operating-margin brand has de-rated to ~3.5x EV/EBITDA
and ~10x forward EPS after guiding its first-ever annual revenue decline. The market is pricing
terminal decline; we underwrite a cyclical trough with an international growth runway, so the
risk/reward is asymmetric (base DCF ~$126 after source-true WACC/capex, target $140 allows
partial multiple recovery).

## Font / color convention (per the assignment)

- **Blue** = figures reported by the company (press release, presentation, filing, or call)
- **Black** = calculations / formulas
- **Red** = analyst assumptions / inputs

The models are *live*: projection cells are Excel formulas driven by the red assumptions, so
changing an assumption re-flows the statements and the valuation.

## Key base-case assumptions (DCF)

- Revenue: −6.1% in FY2026E (guidance midpoint), then +2.3% FY27–30 (StockAnalysis 3Y forecast 2.26%)
- EBIT margin: 13.2% clean/run-rate (Q2 18.8% minus 560bps of tariff refunds), interpolating to 15.5% by FY2030E. FY26 then adds the already-recognized $134.5M IEEPA refund once (~+1.3 ppt on full-year sales, so reported FY26 OM ≈ 14.5%). Do not add the Q2-only +560 bps to the full year.
- Tax rate 30% (FY2026 guidance); capex 5.5% blend (FY26 guided 7.0% is *up* vs FY25 6.1%, then fade); NWC 7.5% of Δrevenue
- WACC 10.5% (CAPM: rf 4.8% from FRED DGS10 4.77% on 2026-09-03, ERP 6.0%, β 0.95, ~100% equity / net cash)
- Terminal growth 2.25%

## Reproducing the outputs

```bash
pip install openpyxl python-pptx
cd scripts
python3 build_3statement.py     # -> ../LULU_3_Statement_Model.xlsx
python3 build_dcf.py            # -> ../LULU_DCF_Valuation_Model.xlsx
python3 build_pitch.py         # -> ../LULU_Investment_Pitch_Deck.pptx
# optional PDF export:
soffice --headless --convert-to pdf ../LULU_Investment_Pitch_Deck.pptx
```

Models were validated by evaluating the workbook formulas (balance sheet ties to zero every
year). FY2026E EPS uses sourced other-income (~$45M from YTD $22.8M) and may sit below
the $9.48–$9.73 guide because we do not plug other income to hit EPS.
