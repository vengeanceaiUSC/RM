## Slide 3 — Investment Thesis Summary

**Descriptor:** Overweight LULU with a $140 target on ~40% upside and an asymmetric payoff

### Three reasons to be long

**1. Priced for terminal decline**  
At ~$100 the stock trades at ~3.5x EV/EBITDA and ~10.4x FY2026E EPS versus a 5-year history of ~20–30x earnings. The balance sheet holds **$1,807M cash** and **$0 funded debt** — nearly the entire enterprise value is operating business at a trough multiple. To justify $100 you need revenue and margins to shrink forever; FY2025 still printed **19.9% operating margin** on **$11.1B revenue**.

**2. International is the multi-year growth engine**  
China Mainland comped **+20%** in FY2025 (+19% constant currency). Rest of World **+9%**. Americas was **−3%** — that is the entire problem, and it is concentrated in one region. Our model returns total revenue to **+2.3%** FY27–30 (StockAnalysis 3Y forecast **2.26%**) after the FY2026 reset.

**3. Elite economics + accretive buybacks**  
FY2025 CFO **$1,602.5M** less capex **$680.8M** → **~$0.9B FCF** in a decelerating year. Repurchases were **$1,178.3M** in FY2025; diluted shares fell **128.0M → 119.1M** (FY2022–FY2025). At $100 every dollar of buyback retires **~2×** the shares it would at prior highs.

**Payoff framing:** Base DCF **$134**; bear **$56** (−44%); bull **$202** (+102%). Downside is cushioned by net cash and an **~8–9% FCF yield**.

## Slide 5 — Macro × Micro: Why LULU Is Performing This Way

**Descriptor:** A discretionary macro air pocket collided with an Americas-specific micro slowdown — the stock prices both at once

*This is the slide that connects the story. Use the 2×2 mentally: macro row, micro column, overlap in the middle.*

### Macro layer (external)

| Force | What it is | How it hits LULU |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| **Consumer discretionary** | Inflation + uncertainty → weaker mall/DTC traffic for premium apparel | 10-K: *"lower store traffic in the Americas, partially reflective of inflationary pressures and economic uncertainty weighing on discretionary purchases"* |
| **Real GDP** | Still growing ~**2.1%** y/y (FRED GDPC1 Q2'26 vs Q2'25: 24,269.6 / 23,771.0) | Not a recession call — a **rotation** call: growth exists, but not in mature U.S. premium athleisure at full price |
| **Tariff regime** | IEEPA and follow-on U.S. tariff layers | **Macro policy → micro P&L:** Q2 recognized **$134.5M refunds** (+560 bps GM) but run-rate margin is **~13.2% clean** (18.8% reported OM minus 560 bps tariff boost) |
| **Competitive macro** | "Athleisure everywhere" + social-commerce brands | Raises marketing spend and narrows perceived differentiation — feeds promotional intensity |

### Micro layer (company-specific)

| Driver | FY25 / latest data | Read-through |
|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Americas comps** | **−3%** FY25; **−12%** Q2 FY26 | Majority of profit pool; traffic, conversion, AOV all pressured |
| **China comps** | **+20%** FY25 (+19% CCY); **−6%** Q2 FY26 | Still the growth engine but **decelerating** as base scales |
| **RoW comps** | **+9%** FY25 (+7% CCY) | Early innings in Europe/APAC |
| **Store fleet** | **811** stores (+44 net FY25); **$1,426** sales/sq ft | Still opening doors while Americas comps negative — productivity math matters |
| **Channel mix** | Stores **$5.05B** / E-comm **$4.92B** / Other **$1.13B** | DTC-heavy model = full-price economics when traffic cooperates |
| **Inventory / promo** | Inventories **$1.70B** on COGS **$4.82B** → **~129 DIO** | Working capital build; markdown language in 10-K supports our −1 day/yr DIO path |
| **SG&A deleverage** | FY26 guide on **lower revenue** with fixed store base | Operating margin trough in FY26 before recovery |

### The overlap (why the stock is at $100)

1. **Macro weakness concentrated in micro geography:** Americas is ~**71%** of FY25 revenue — the macro consumer tape hits LULU harder than a globally diversified peer because of **where** the dollars are earned.

2. **Tariff is both macro and micro:** Policy risk sits in the macro bucket; the **$134.5M refund** sits in the micro P&L. Investors who miss the 560 bps Q2-only boost overstate sustainable margin.

3. **Guide vs. history:** The market extrapolated one bad guide into permanence. Macro did not collapse — **LULU's Americas comp did**. Those are related but not identical.

4. **Valuation absorbs overlap:** At ~10x FY26 EPS the stock no longer requires a macro boom **or** positive Americas comps to work — it requires **stabilization**.

5. **Our edge:** Separate **run-rate** (13.2% clean EBIT margin) from **reported** (15.2% FY26 in our IS, incl. refund) from **peak** (19.9% FY25). The DCF underwrites trough economics, not recovery to peak.

**GIS talking point:** *"We're not pitching a return to 30x earnings. We're pitching that terminal decline is wrong."*

## Slide 9 — Industry Overview

**Descriptor:** Structural tailwinds favor scaled brands in a consolidating category

- Global activewear **~$400B**, mid-single-digit growth — wellness + casualization
- Fragmented category consolidating toward **scale + technical product + omnichannel**
- Barriers rising: brand equity, fabric R&D, supply chain, productive retail footprint

**Where LULU fits**
- Premium, category-defining technical apparel — **structurally higher margins** than peers
- **Under-indexed internationally** vs Nike/adidas — primary multi-year lever
- Competition real (Alo, Vuori, On) but LULU's scale + innovation cadence remain differentiated
